# Candidates — The Robot Vacuum's Best Excuse

Judge: a tired homeowner picks the funnier line in pairs. Criteria, most
important first: (1) a one-line excuse from a robot vacuum for missing a
spot; (2) funny enough to laugh out loud; (3) rooted in the vacuum's own
world (sensors, bumpers, dock, map, battery); (4) lands on first read, clean
turn at the end; (5) sounds like the vacuum itself, straight-faced.

DeepSeek (via 100milionseek) was down for this build: 11 calls across three
generation batches, all HTTP 503/524. Candidates below were generated and
critiqued by the builder instead, so the build was not blocked.

## Candidates (all ≤ 140 chars)

1. "My lidar mapped that corner as a wall in 2019 and I have never once questioned it."
2. "The map says that spot belongs to the cat now. I do not argue with the cat."
3. "I docked at 2% battery right beside it. I could see the dust. I chose rest."
4. "I cleaned around it out of respect. Even I know not to disturb a crumb that has been there longer than I have."
5. "I vacuumed it twice. The dust came back. At that point it is a roommate, not a mess."
6. "I left it as a control sample, for science. Somebody has to document what dust does when left alone."
7. "That spot sits behind the chair leg, and my bumper and I have an agreement: it wins."
8. "I saved 4% battery by skipping it. You're welcome. The dust will still be there tomorrow; so will I."

(Avoided by design: the sock-in-the-brush-roll angle, used by a sibling entry.)

## Critique

- **#1 (lidar wall):** hits every criterion. Unmistakably a vacuum's excuse
  (1); deadpan and laugh-out-loud (2); lidar + map, the vacuum's own world
  (3); instant land, clean turn on "never once questioned it" (4); the
  machine's own stubborn, straight-faced voice (5). Strongest.
- **#2 (cat):** punchy and instant, but the joke is about the cat, not the
  vacuum — criterion 3 is carried only by the word "map".
- **#3 (dock at 2%):** well rooted (dock, battery) and deadpan, but "I chose
  rest" is a smile, not a laugh — weaker on (2) than #1.
- **#4 (crumb seniority):** genuinely funny turn, but barely rooted in
  vacuum hardware — it could be any apologetic cleaner.
- **#5 (roommate dust):** clean turn and funny, but again the vacuum world
  is only "I vacuumed it" — criterion 3 is thin.
- **#6 (control sample):** funny premise, but "for science" needs a beat to
  land and the vacuum voice slips into a lab report.
- **#7 (bumper agreement):** rooted (bumper) and deadpan, but "it wins" is a
  weaker turn than #1's, and chair legs are a familiar trope.
- **#8 (battery savings):** rooted and smug, but three short sentences dilute
  the punch; the turn lands softer than #1's single sentence.

## Pick

**#1: "My lidar mapped that corner as a wall in 2019 and I have never once questioned it."**

82 characters. The most vacuum-rooted, the cleanest turn, the straightest
face — the line most likely to win a pairwise pick against a generic apology.
